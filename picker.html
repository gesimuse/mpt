MPT video picker

Pick one of the recent aibeauty images (or upload your own), edit the motion prompt, press Confirm. That fires autopilot_video.yml in the repo; the mp4 lands in your TikTok inbox as a draft (~10 min later).

One-time setup (fine-grained PAT)

Create a fine-grained PAT at github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new → scope it to only this repo (gesimuse/mpt) → Repository permissions: set Actions to Read and write and Contents to Read and write (Contents is needed to upload your own images). Paste the token below and press Verify; it stays in your browser's localStorage on this device only.
